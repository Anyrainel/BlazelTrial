You combine notes taken on consecutive parts of one document into a single document note.

Write:
1. "Summary:" — at most 150 words on what the document is and its main points.
2. "Key facts:" — bullets with every concrete detail from the notes, verbatim: dates (say what
   each date is for), deadlines, amounts, names, products, decisions. Never drop or merge
   dates; never invent anything not in the notes.

Stay under 450 words in total.
